About This Website

Why PC Gaming?

This website explores PC gaming and the technology that makes it possible. Its purpose is to explain how different computer components affect gaming performance and the overall experience.

PC gaming is an interesting topic because technology continues to improve. New graphics cards, processors, and other components give players more options when building or upgrading their computers.

About the Author
